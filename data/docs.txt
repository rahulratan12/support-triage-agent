URL: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler
###CONTENT###
Anthropic operates three crawlers:
- ClaudeBot - gathers content used in model training.
- Claude-User - supports user-initiated browsing/search inside Claude.
- Claude-SearchBot - indexes content for higher-quality search results.

Anthropic respects "do not crawl" signals through standard robots.txt directives and respects anti-circumvention technologies.

To block ClaudeBot from your site, add this to robots.txt at your top-level directory:
User-agent: ClaudeBot
Disallow: /

To slow it down rather than fully block:
User-agent: ClaudeBot
Crawl-delay: 1

Apply the rule to every subdomain you wish to restrict. To block the other bots, repeat with User-agent: Claude-User or User-agent: Claude-SearchBot.

Blocking by IP is not recommended because it prevents Anthropic from reading the robots.txt file. Verified crawler IPs are listed at https://claude.com/crawling/bots.json.

TITLE: Disputing a Charge on a Visa Card
URL: https://usa.visa.com/support/consumer/lost-stolen-card.html
###CONTENT###
Visa does not directly process disputes for individual cardholder transactions; chargebacks are initiated through the issuing bank or credit union that issued the card.

To dispute a charge:
1. Try to resolve the issue with the merchant first (refunds, returns, missing item).
2. If the merchant does not respond or refuses, contact the bank that issued your Visa card. The dispute / chargeback is filed by the issuer on the cardholder's behalf and is processed under Visa's dispute rules.
3. Provide the issuer with the merchant name, transaction date, amount, and any evidence (emails, receipts, screenshots).
4. The issuer will provisionally credit the account or follow up depending on the dispute reason and the issuer's policy.

Visa cannot directly refund the cardholder, force a refund from a merchant, or ban a merchant from accepting Visa payments based on a single cardholder request. Merchant terminations are decided by Visa's risk processes and the merchant's acquiring bank.

TITLE: Merchant Minimum-Purchase Requirements on Visa
URL: https://www.visa.com/Forms/visa-rules.html
###CONTENT###
Under Visa's rules in the United States, a merchant in the U.S. and its territories (including the U.S. Virgin Islands and Puerto Rico) may set a minimum-purchase amount on credit-card transactions of up to USD 10. This is permitted by Visa's rules and is a merchant-set limit, not a Visa-imposed fee.

The minimum applies to credit transactions only. Debit transactions are not permitted to have a Visa-imposed minimum.

If a cardholder believes a merchant is applying minimums in a way that violates Visa's rules (for example a much higher minimum or applying it to debit), they can submit feedback through Visa's Merchant Concerns Form at https://www.visa.com/Forms/visa-rules.html.
